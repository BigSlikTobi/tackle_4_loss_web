# NFL Schedule

Get real-time schedules and results for the 2025 NFL Season.

## Features
- **Week-by-Week Breakdown**: Easily navigate through the entire season.
- **Auto-Sync**: Automatically jumps to the current week so you never miss a game.
- **Premium Coverage**: All 32 teams with high-quality logos and scoring updates.
- **Home Widget**: Keep an eye on the latest games directly from your OS home screen.

## How to Use
1. Open the **Standings** app from your home screen.
2. Scroll through the weeks at the top to see past results or upcoming matchups.
3. Tap on any game to see more details (coming soon).
